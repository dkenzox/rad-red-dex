What does uploading your save do?

Uploading your save is used with Ability Randomizer and Learnset Randomizer. The general pokemon info will change to match your specific randomizer seed.

Filters

Name: search for a species by name.

Type: Show all species with a certain type.

Move: Show all species who can learn a certain move.

Ability: Show all species who can have a certain ability.
